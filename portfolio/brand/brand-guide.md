# Zaphira — brand guide

## Essence

**Handmade luxury bags from Algiers — Picasso & the Mediterranean.**
Motto: *Art · Créativité · Confiance* (Art · Creativity · Confidence).
The house is feminine, calm and sure of itself: a single confident line, the blue of the sea, gold details.

## Typography

- **Headings:** Cormorant Garamond (serif), regular or italic, normal letter-spacing.
- **Interface and labels:** Plus Jakarta Sans; small uppercase labels with wide letter-spacing (0.14–0.2em).
- **Arabic:** Amiri for headings, IBM Plex Sans Arabic for text.

## Motifs

Six single-line drawings in gold, after Picasso's portraits: a woman's face, a profile, a wave, an eye, an olive branch, a Moorish arch and the sun. They draw themselves when they scroll into view. Never copy an actual Picasso artwork.

## Photography

Real Zaphira bags only, in rectangular frames with the same proportions everywhere (3:4 for products). No photos from other brands or from Pinterest.

## Packaging

**Signature Picasso Blue** (`packaging-picasso-blue.svg`): a blue shopping bag with beige ribbon handles and a gold line-art portrait; a blue box with a beige band and the gold Z; a matching card.

## Voice

Warm, precise, never loud. Speak of hands, time and places (Tipaza, the Casbah, Djurdjura). French first, English and Arabic equally cared for.
